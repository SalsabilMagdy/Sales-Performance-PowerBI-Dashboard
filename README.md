# 📊 Sales Performance Power BI Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze overall business sales performance, evaluate profitability metrics, and track Year-over-Year (YOY) revenue growth. This project demonstrates data modeling, custom DAX measure development, and dynamic data visualization.

---

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) & Highlights
* **Revenue Metrics:** Tracked Net Revenue, Total Sales Quantity, Discount Amount, and Discount %.
* **Product Averages:** Computed Average Revenue per Product and Average Sales Quantity per Product.
* **YOY Growth:** Comprehensive Year-over-Year (YOY) Revenue Analysis to measure business expansion.
* **Regional & Category Breakdown:** Evaluated revenue distribution across different geographic regions and product categories.
* **Time Series Trends:** Visualized monthly and yearly sales performance trends for historical comparisons.
* **Interactivity:** Integrated dynamic slicers for **Year**, **Quarter**, and **Region**.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills Used
* **Power BI Desktop:** Dashboard design and layout structuring.
* **Power Query:** Data extraction, transformation, and cleaning (ETL).
* **Data Modeling:** Star Schema design, table relationships, and data structuring.
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Custom calculated measures for KPIs, growth percentages, and time intelligence functions.

---

## 💡 What I Learned
* Writing Time Intelligence DAX functions to perform accurate YOY growth calculations.
* Building clear star-schema data models for efficient cross-filtering performance.
* Structuring interactive visual hierarchies tailored for executive decision-making.
